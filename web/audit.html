Audit

An audit is a formal, independent examination and evaluation of an organization’s systems, processes, records, or performance. Its purpose is to verify accuracy, ensure compliance with established standards or laws, and identify areas for improvement or risk.

Common Types of Audits

  • Financial Audit
  • Information Security / IT Audit
  • Compliance / Regulatory Audit
  • Operational Audit

Standard Audit Phases

  1. Planning & Scoping
  2. Fieldwork & Evidence Gathering
  3. Analysis & Finding Gathering
  4. Reporting
  5. Remediation
