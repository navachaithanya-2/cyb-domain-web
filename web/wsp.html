Company's Written Supervisory Procedures(WSP)

Written Supervisory Procedures (WSPs) are formal, documented internal guidelines and operating rules that financial firms (particularly broker-dealers and registered investment advisers) establish to ensure compliance with securities laws, regulations, and industry standards.

In regulated financial environments (such as under FINRA Rule 3110 or SEC regulations), firms are required to establish, maintain, and enforce a supervisory system reasonably designed to achieve compliance. The WSP document serves as the operational blueprint for that system.

Core Components of a WSP Documents

  1. Supervisory Structure & Chain of Command
  2. Compliance & Operational controls
  3. Cybersecuiryt & Data Privacy
  4. Review Frequency & Evidence of Supervision
  5. Annual Review & Maintenance

Key Regulatory Considerations

  • FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision)
  • SEC Rule 206(4)-7 (Investment Advisers Act)
  • Enforceability & Practical Application
