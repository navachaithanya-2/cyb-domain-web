Vulnerability Management

Vulnerability Management is the continuous, proactive practice of identifying, classifying, prioritizing, remediating, and mitigating software flaws, misconfigurations, and security weaknesses across an organization's IT ecosystem.

Unlike a one-off vulnerability scan, vulnerability management is an ongoing lifecycle designed to stay ahead of new threats and minimize the attack surface.

  • The Vulnerability Management Lifecycle

    1. Asset Discovery & Inventory
    2. Vulnerability Assessment
    3. Risk Prioritization
    4. Remediation & Mitigation
    5. Verification & Continuous Monitoring

  • Core Prioritization Metrics

    • CVSS (Common Vulnerability Scoring System)
    • EPSS (Exploit Prediction Scoring System)
    • Asset Criticality
