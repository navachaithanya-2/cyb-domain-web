Recovery

Recovery is the phase in cybersecurity and business continuity where an organization restores systems, data, and operations back to normal functioning following an outage, disaster, or cyberattack (such as a ransomware incident).

Within defensive frameworks, recovery ensures that even after a major disruption or security breach, critical operations can be resumed with minimal downtime and data loss.

  • Core Objectives of Recovery

    • Business Continuity
    • Data Restoration
    • Integrity Validation

  • Key Recovery Metrics

    • RPO(Recovery Point Objective)
    • RTO(Recovery Time Objective)

  • Key Components of Strong Recovery Plan

    • The 3-2-1 Backip Strategy
    • Disaster Recovery(DR) Sites
      • Hot Site
      • Warm Site
      • Cold Site
    • Regular Recovery Testing
