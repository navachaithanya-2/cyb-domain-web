Law and Regulations

In governance, laws and regulations establish the mandatory legal and regulatory boundaries within which organizations must operate. They set the legal baseline for accountability, transparency, risk management, and the protection of stakeholders, consumers, and data.

While internal governance policies define how an organization chooses to manage itself, laws and regulations determine what it must do to avoid civil liability, regulatory penalties, or criminal prosecution.

Key Areas of Regulatory Governance

  • Data Privacy & Consumer Protection
  • Cybersecurity & Incident Reporting
  • Industry-Specific Compliance
  • Ethics, Bribery & Competition
