Governance

In cybersecurity and enterprise IT, governance is the overarching framework of rules, practices, policies, and organizational structures that ensures an organization’s security strategies align directly with its broader business objectives, adhere to legal and regulatory mandates, and manage risk responsibly.

While operational security focuses on tactical execution (e.g., configuring firewalls, deploying EDR, hunting threats), governance establishes the authority, accountability, and boundaries within which those technical systems operate.
