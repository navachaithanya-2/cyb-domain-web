Assets Inventory

An asset inventory is a comprehensive, up-to-date catalog of all the hardware, software, data, and digital resources owned or used by an organization. It is widely considered the foundational first step in cybersecurity, IT management, and compliance—because you cannot protect what you do not know you have.
