Incident Response

Incident Response (IR) is the structured, systematic approach an organization follows to prepare for, detect, contain, and recover from a cybersecurity incident, data breach, or system disruption.

Its primary goal is to minimize damage, reduce recovery time, preserve forensic evidence, and lower the financial and operational impact of an attack.

The Incident Response Lifecycle (NIST SP 800-61)

  1. Preparation
  2. Detection & Analysis
  3. Containment, Eradication & Recovery
  4. Post-Incident Activity

Core Roles in an Incident Response Team (CSIRT)

  • Incident Commander / IR Lead
  • Security Analysts & Forensics Investigators
  • IT / Infrastructure Engineers
  • Legal & Compliance
  • Communications & PR
