Security Operations

Security Operations (often centered around a Security Operations Center, or SOC) is the ongoing practice of monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cybersecurity threats across an organization's systems and networks.

While engineering teams design and build defenses, operations teams act as the front line—maintaining 24/7 visibility over digital assets to catch and contain intrusions.
