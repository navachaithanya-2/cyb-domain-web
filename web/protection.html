Protection

In cybersecurity and IT, protection refers to the controls, tools, and practices deployed to defend systems, networks, devices, and data from unauthorized access, damage, or compromise.

Following the broader sequence of defensive architecture, it sits alongside detection and response to keep digital assets resilient.
