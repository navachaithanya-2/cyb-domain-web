Security Engineering

Security Engineering is the specialized discipline within cybersecurity focused on designing, building, and maintaining systems, networks, and software so they remain dependable and resilient—even when facing malicious attacks or operational failures.


While cybersecurity analysts often monitor and respond to incidents, security engineers are the architects and builders who ensure defenses are embedded into the infrastructure from day one.
